# Phase 3 shot list: city plates and depth

Why: the seven city photographs were taken at different times of day and in different conditions, and each was
drawn as one flat sheet. Several read as flat, noisy or broken once graded to night. This list replaces the
weak ones with clean night plates and gives every city a depth pass, so near and far buildings move apart as
the camera moves.

Tool: Higgsfield, Nano Banana 2.1. Every plate is made **from the licensed photograph as a reference**, so the
real buildings, bridges and landmarks stay where they are. Nothing is invented from text alone.

## Shots

Each night plate: same camera position and framing as its reference; clear, empty, deep-navy sky (the
fireworks are drawn live, so the sky must be clean); lit windows and floodlighting; real reflections where
there is water; light haze between near and far for depth; no people, boats in motion, text or lens flare.
4K, 3:2.

## How the layers are used

- **Sky**: far away, with the skyline filled in behind it (as now).
- **City**: no longer one flat sheet. The depth pass places every part of the photograph at its own distance,
  so the wheel, the bridge or the tower stands in front of what is behind it.
- **Water**: a real horizontal plane with live reflections (as now).
- **Fireworks, smoke, haze**: live, between the layers (as now).

Each result is checked against its reference before use. A plate that changes a landmark is retaken or
dropped in favour of the original photograph.
