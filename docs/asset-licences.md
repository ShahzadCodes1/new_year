## Notes

- No licence here includes a warranty or a property release. See `phase-1-shot-list.md` for the reasoning and the option of a paid, warranted image for production.
- Generated plates are outputs of the account holder's Higgsfield plan. Before launch, confirm that plan's terms allow commercial use of outputs.
- Local processing (`scripts/build-plates.py`): resizing, a sky mask, a filled sky behind the skyline, and a relief from the depth pass. A three-pixel light line was trimmed from the top edge of the London file.
- The Birmingham photograph was checked to be Birmingham, England, not Birmingham, Alabama.
- Higgsfield credits spent: 78 of the 90 approved (34 on city plates and depth, see `phase-3-shot-list.md`; 10 on chapter 4; 20 on chapter 5; 14 on chapter 6).
